### Task 1: Custom categories

**Files:** `app/finbot/categories.py`, `app/finbot/texts.py`, `app/finbot/bot/handlers.py`, `app/tests/test_categories.py`, `app/tests/test_texts.py`, `app/tests/test_bot.py`.

1. `/category add <name>` adds an expense category; `/category add income <name>` adds an income one. The name is the rest of the line, trimmed, 1-30 characters, with at least one letter (any alphabet), not starting with `/`. It goes last in its kind (position = the kind's max + 1), so it shows last in the category buttons.
   - Reply: `Added expense category Rent.` / `Added income category Bonus.`
   - Same name already exists in either kind (case-insensitive): `There is already a category Rent.`
   - Bad or missing name: `Use: /category add Rent or /category add income Bonus`
   - At most 30 categories per kind: `Up to 30 expense categories. Rename one instead: /category rename Old New` (income: `income`).
2. `/category rename <old> <new>`: `<old>` is the longest leading run of words that matches an existing category name (case-insensitive), `<new>` is the rest, with the same name rules as add. `/category rename Eating out Food` renames "Eating out" to "Food".
   - Reply: `Renamed Eating out to Food.`
   - No leading words match: `No category named Eating. See /category`
   - New name taken or invalid: as in item 1 (`There is already a category Food.` / `Use: /category rename Rent Housing`).
   - Keywords and entries keep pointing at the same category id.
3. `/category` with no words lists both kinds in position order:
   ```
   Expense: Groceries, Eating out, ..., Rent
   Income: Salary, Freelance, Other income
   Add: /category add Rent · Rename: /category rename Rent Housing
   ```
   Any other words: `Use: /category add Rent or /category rename Rent Housing`.
4. Categories cannot be deleted (entries refer to them).
5. Add `categories.find_category(conn, name: str) -> Category | None` (either kind, case-insensitive, trimmed); add, rename and Task 2 use it.
6. Tests: add both kinds, position last, the new category appears in the category keyboard of a new word; duplicate across kinds and case; name rules (empty, 31 characters, digits only, leading `/`); the 30 cap; rename with a two-word old name, unknown old, taken new name; after a rename the remembered keyword maps to the renamed category and `/month` shows the new name.

### Task 2: Monthly budgets per expense category

**Files:** `app/finbot/db.py`, `app/finbot/budgets.py` (new), `app/finbot/reports.py`, `app/finbot/texts.py`, `app/finbot/bot/handlers.py`, `app/tests/test_db.py`, `app/tests/test_budgets.py` (new), `app/tests/test_reports.py`, `app/tests/test_texts.py`, `app/tests/test_bot.py`.

1. Migration 2 -> 3: `CREATE TABLE budgets (category_id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES categories(id), amount_cents INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK (amount_cents > 0))`, then `user_version = 3`. Test it on a version-2 database with data, and the locked re-check as for 1 -> 2.
2. A budget is a monthly limit in EUR for one expense category and applies to every month until changed. Spent = the sum of the EUR amounts of that category's non-deleted expenses dated in that month; pending entries (no rate yet) are not counted.
3. `/budget <category> <amount>`: the last word is the amount (`parse_amount`, EUR), the words before it are the category (case-insensitive, may have several words). Amount 0 removes the budget.
   - Set: `Rent: 850.00 EUR a month. November: spent 0.00, left 850.00.` (current month, today's date from the dispatcher)
   - Removed: `Rent: budget removed.`; removing a budget that does not exist: `Rent has no budget.`
   - Income category: `Budgets are for expense categories.`
   - Unknown category: `No category named Rnt. See /category`
   - Bad shape or amount: `Use: /budget Rent 850.00 (0 removes it)`
4. `/budget` with no words lists the current month, categories in position order:
   ```
   Budgets, November 2026:
   Rent: 800.00 of 850.00 · 50.00 left
   Eating out: 320.00 of 300.00 · over by 20.00
   ```
   No budgets: `No budgets yet. Set one: /budget Rent 850.00`. Exactly at the limit: `· 0.00 left`.
5. The saved reply of an expense in a budgeted category gets one more line, for the entry's own month (not today's): `Rent: 800.00 of 850.00 in November · 50.00 left` or `· over by 50.00`. The same line appears when the reply is redrawn after picking a category, and after changing the category. A pending entry gets no budget line. Income and pot moves get none.
6. `/month` gets a `Budgets` block after the category block, only when budgets exist, one line per budget in position order, same format as `/budget` lines (`Rent: 800.00 of 850.00 · 50.00 left`), for the report's month.
7. Tests: spent per month boundary (31.10 vs 01.11), USD entry counted by its EUR amount, pending not counted, deleted not counted, other categories not counted; over and exact; the saved-reply line for a back-dated entry uses that entry's month; pick and change category redraw it; income category refused; multi-word category; remove; `/month` block present and absent.

Commit: `feat: monthly budgets per expense category`

### Task 3: Clear a month with confirmation

**Files:** `app/finbot/ledger.py`, `app/finbot/texts.py`, `app/finbot/bot/keyboards.py`, `app/finbot/bot/handlers.py`, `app/tests/test_ledger.py`, `app/tests/test_texts.py`, `app/tests/test_bot.py`.

1. `/clear` (current month), `/clear 10`, `/clear 2026-10`: the month argument works as in `/month` (`reports.parse_month_arg`, same error text).
2. It counts the month's non-deleted expenses and incomes (by entry date). Pot moves are never cleared.
   - None: `Nothing to clear in November 2026.`
   - Otherwise the bot asks: `Delete all 12 expenses and incomes of November 2026? Savings stay.` with one row of buttons `Delete 12 entries` and `Cancel` (one entry: `Delete all 1 expense or income of November 2026? Savings stay.` and `Delete 1 entry`).
3. Confirm: in one `with conn:`, count again; if the count is unchanged, soft-delete exactly those entries and edit the message to `Deleted 12 entries of November 2026.`; if it changed, delete nothing and edit to `November 2026 changed since you asked. Send /clear again.` Cancel: edit to `Nothing deleted.` Both remove the buttons. A second press on a confirm button after the delete finds a count of 0, which differs, so nothing more is deleted.
4. Callback data `ClearCb` (prefix `c`) carries action, year, month and the count shown.
5. Tests: counts by month boundary, kinds (expenses and incomes cleared; save/take untouched, pot totals unchanged), already-deleted entries ignored; confirm deletes and reports; cancel; changed count refuses; double press; `/clear 13` error text; empty month; the entries are soft-deleted (ids kept).

Commit: `feat: clear a month after confirmation`
