# Verification: Telegram personal-finance products (checked 31 Oct 2026)

Checks the notes in `telegram_finance_products.md` (same folder). The notes file was not modified.

## Method and limits

- Telegram handles: static t.me pages fetched with curl (meta tags only). Nine handles checked.
- Product, article and bank pages: WebFetch. Answers come from a model summary; quoted phrases are as returned. Exact wording is given where it matters.
- GitHub: `gh api` for stars, last push, license and archived status of 16 repos. READMEs read for 6 repos.
- WebSearch: 5 calls used (budget about 12).
- Not checked: Reddit, Habr, vc.ru, Capterra, Gumroad, tapps.center, findmini.app, Spendly, Copilka, Cashy, Telegram Mini App pages (t.me/bot/app), TrackMyDollar, GoSavingsBot's README, IGNIO's own site.
- Blocked: root-nation.com returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch. api.monobank.ua/docs/swagger.json returned HTTP 403 to curl. Neither was bypassed. Both are marked UNVERIFIABLE.

Verdict key: CONFIRMED = matches the cited source. WRONG = the source gives a different value (correct value given). PARTLY WRONG = part matches, part does not. INCOMPLETE = the claim is not false but misses a fact the source shows. UNVERIFIABLE = no source reachable or none found (reason given).

## Overall reliability of the notes

Rating: MEDIUM. Product facts and GitHub data are reliable; bank-side conclusions are weak.
Why (product and GitHub): all nine t.me handles checked exist, and all 13 repo records (stars, last push, license) match the GitHub API. Prices for Cointry, Tabby, IGNIO and Expenselizer match their pages.
Why (errors): six items are wrong or overstated: Cointry budgets are Premium only; Cointry free multi-currency is not confirmed; Cointry's "2026" is a copyright footer; the Expenselizer operator is named on Product Hunt; the camt.053 claim is not on the cited page; the private George export formats are not on the cited page. Four are incomplete or do not exist: the Tabby operator is named on Product Hunt; TeleExpense usage is understated (29 subscriptions, TrustMRR); two listed repos do not exist (404).
Why (bank side): the "no Erste/George or PrivatBank import" conclusion rests on empty searches, and the one bank-format detail checked was wrong. Treat the bank section as low reliability until the George and Monobank docs are read directly.
