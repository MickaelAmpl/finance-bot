### Task 1: Several entries in one message

**Files:** `app/finbot/parser.py`, `app/finbot/texts.py`, `app/finbot/bot/handlers.py`, `app/tests/test_parser.py`, `app/tests/test_texts.py`, `app/tests/test_bot.py`.

1. Splitting (in `parser.py`, private helper): the message text is split into parts.
   - Every line is a part; lines are stripped; empty lines are skipped.
   - Inside a line, a comma followed by one or more spaces splits the line only when the text after the spaces starts an entry: an optional `+`, an optional currency symbol from `money.SYMBOLS` (`€ $ ₴`), then a digit; or the word `save` or `take` (any case) followed by a space. The comma itself belongs to neither part.
   - So `850.00 rent, 120 utilities, 30 internet` is 3 parts; `+3000 salary, 30 internet` is 2; `save 200 emergency, 30 internet` is 2; `25 coffee, croissant` is 1 part with the note `coffee, croissant`; `12,50 coffee` and `1 200,50 taxi` are 1 part (no space after the comma), parsed as today.
2. `parser.parse_entries(text: str, today: date) -> list[Entry]`:
   - 0 or 1 part: `[parse_entry(text, today)]`, so every current reply and error is unchanged.
   - More than 10 parts: `ParseError("Up to 10 entries in one message. Nothing saved.")`.
   - Otherwise every part goes through `parse_entry` in order. If part N fails, nothing is returned and the error is `ParseError(f'Nothing saved. Entry {N} "{part}": {error}')`, where `part` longer than 40 characters is cut to its first 39 characters plus `…`.
3. `handlers.on_entry_text` uses `parse_entries`. A parse error answers its text once, nothing is saved.
4. Several entries: first save all of them in order, building each reply right after its own save (the same reply a single message gets today: `saved_text` with its budget line and the category or entry keyboard, or `pot_text` for save/take). Then send the replies in order, one message per entry. Saving first means a failed send can never leave later entries unsaved.
   - A `SavingsError` on entry N (for example taking more than the pot has) does not stop the others: its reply is `f"Entry {N} not saved: {error}"`, in its place in the order.
   - One entry: exactly today's flow and replies (a `SavingsError` answers `str(error)` with no prefix).
5. Budget lines: each entry's line is computed right after that entry is saved, so `100 groceries` then `50 groceries` in one message show spent 100 and then 150 (same as two separate messages).
6. `HELP`: after the line `save 200 emergency - add 200 EUR to the emergency pot` add `850.00 rent, 120 utilities - several entries (or one per line)`. Nothing else in HELP changes.
7. Tests:
   - Parser: three lines -> 3 entries with the right amounts and notes; the comma form -> 3; blank lines ignored; `25 coffee, croissant` one entry with note `coffee, croissant`; `12,50 coffee` one entry of 1250 cents; `+3000 salary, 30 internet` income then expense; `€5 tip, $3 gum` EUR then USD; `save 200 emergency, 30 internet` pot move then expense; `coffee 25` alone still raises the plain `Start with the amount, e.g. 25 coffee`; 10 parts accepted, 11 refused with the exact text; part 2 bad -> the exact `Nothing saved. Entry 2 "...": ...` text; a 50-character bad part is quoted cut to 39 characters plus `…`.
   - Bot: a three-line message -> 3 replies in order, 3 live entries in the database, each reply with its own keyboard; the comma form with a budgeted category shows the budget line on that entry; two entries of one budgeted category show spent growing (item 5); one bad part -> one reply, no entry saved; `take` more than the pot has in the middle -> the other two saved, the middle reply `Entry 2 not saved: ...`; the Undo button of the second reply deletes only the second entry; a single entry message is unchanged (one existing test is enough to show it).
   - Texts: the new HELP line in its place.

## Review Focus

- One-entry behaviour unchanged (reply text, buttons, errors).
- No entry is saved when any part fails to parse; nothing is lost when a reply fails to send.
- The comma rule never splits an amount like `12,50` or `1 200,50`.

## Known risk (accepted)

- Up to 10 replies are sent quickly to one chat. Telegram may slow a burst down; a failed send is logged, the entries are already saved and `/last` shows them.
- A note with a comma before a number (`25 lunch, 2 people`) now becomes two entries (`2 people` is saved as 2.00). Each reply shows what was saved and has its Undo button.
- A note written on two lines (`25 coffee` then `with milk` on the next line) is now refused: `Nothing saved. Entry 2 "with milk": ...`. Loud, not silent; write the note on one line.
