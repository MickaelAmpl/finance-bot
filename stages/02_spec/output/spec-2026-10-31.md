# Finance Bot - product spec

Status: approved by Misha on 2026-10-31 ("План полностью утверждаю", plan page v2). This English spec is the agent copy of that plan; items marked ASSUMPTION are product-owner readings inside the approved scope, listed in `HANDOFF.md` for Misha to confirm.

Approved source: `plan-page-v2-2026-10-31.html` (https://claude.ai/artifact/FjKWD1zkf6SZskBxFVjcgW).

## Product

A Telegram bot for one owner (Misha). He types one line per expense, income or savings move. On the 1st of each month the bot sends a summary. Phase 2 adds bill splitting in group chats with friends. Phase 3 adds a Mini App for charts and complex splits. Runs on Misha's VPS server. Interface language: English.

## Phase 1 - personal ledger (target: in use from 6.11.2026)

### Input

- R1. Expense: `<amount> [currency] <note words> [date]`. Examples: `25 coffee`, `12,50 lunch`, `€25 coffee`, `25€ coffee`, `25 usd book`, `1200 uah taxi`.
- R2. Income: a leading `+` attached to the amount: `+3000 salary`.
- R3. Amount: positive, at most 2 decimals, `.` or `,` as decimal separator, no thousands separators, maximum 10,000,000.00. Invalid amounts are rejected with an example of correct input.
- R4. Currency: EUR (default when omitted), USD, UAH. Codes are case-insensitive; symbols `€`, `$`, `₴` may be attached to the amount or follow it as a separate token. A known but unsupported code (GBP, CHF, PLN, CZK, HUF, TRY, JPY) is rejected with "Use EUR, USD or UAH".
- R5. Note: at least one word after the amount (and currency). The first word, lowercased, is the keyword used for the category. The full note is stored as typed.
- R6. Date: optional last token `yesterday`, `DD.MM` or `DD.MM.YYYY`. Default: today in Europe/Vienna. A `DD.MM` later than today means last year. Invalid dates (31.02) are rejected.
- R7. Input that looks like a split number (`1 200 taxi`) is rejected with "Write 1200 without spaces".

### Categories

- R8. Fixed defaults. Expense: Groceries, Eating out, Transport, Housing, Utilities, Health, Shopping, Entertainment, Travel, Subscriptions, Gifts, Other. Income: Salary, Freelance, Other income. Custom categories are out of scope for phase 1 (backlog).
- R9. Known keyword: category is set automatically. Unknown keyword: the entry is saved immediately as uncategorized and the bot shows category buttons of the entry's kind; the tap sets the category and remembers keyword -> category.
- R10. Change category (button on the confirmation, or from `/last`) also updates the remembered keyword.

### Confirmation

- R11. Reply format:
  - `Saved: -25.00 EUR · coffee → Eating out` with buttons `[Change category] [Undo]`.
  - Foreign currency: `Saved: -1,200.00 UAH (≈ -26.40 EUR at 0.0220) · taxi → Transport`.
  - Income: `Saved: +3,000.00 EUR · salary → Salary`.
  - Unknown keyword: `Saved: -42.00 EUR · dm`, next line `New word "dm" - pick a category:`, plus category buttons.
  Amounts are formatted with a comma thousands separator and 2 decimals; the sign is ASCII `-` or `+`.

### Exchange rates

- R12. `/rate <usd|uah> <eur_per_unit>`, e.g. `/rate usd 0.92`: 1 USD = 0.92 EUR. Rate > 0, at most 6 decimals. Stored with today's date; history is kept.
- R13. A foreign entry uses the latest rate set on or before the entry date; the rate is frozen into the entry.
- R14. No rate yet: the entry is saved as pending, the reply adds `No USD rate yet. Send /rate usd <EUR per 1 USD> and I will convert it.`. Setting the rate converts all pending entries of that currency and replies how many were converted. Pending entries are excluded from totals and reports show their count.

### Savings pots

- R15. `save <amount> <pot>` adds to a pot (created on first save); `take <amount> <pot>` withdraws. Pot names are one word, case-insensitive. Pot moves are not expenses or income. ASSUMPTION: pots are in EUR only in phase 1; a currency other than EUR in `save`/`take` is rejected.
- R16. `take` more than the pot balance, or from an unknown pot, is rejected.
- R17. `/goal <pot> <amount>` sets a target. `/savings` shows each pot: total, goal and percent if set, last 5 moves with dates.
- R18. Reply: `Emergency pot: +200.00 EUR · total 1,400.00 EUR`.

### Corrections

- R19. `/undo` removes the latest entry (transaction or pot move) and says what was removed. `[Undo]` on a confirmation removes that entry. Removal is a soft delete.
- R20. `/last` lists the last 10 entries with buttons per entry: change category, delete.

### Reports

- R21. `/month` (current month), `/month 10` (October of the current year, or last year if that month is in the future), `/month 2026-10`.
- R22. Report content, amounts in EUR:
  - Income, Spent (with change vs previous month: `▲ 6% vs Sep` / `▼ 6% vs Sep`, omitted when the previous month had no spending), Saved (net pot moves of the month), Net = Income - Spent - Saved.
  - Expense categories sorted by amount: name, amount, bar of `█` (12 chars for the largest, at least 1), percent of Spent (rounded).
  - Top 5 expenses: date, amount, note.
  - Savings: each pot `total / goal (pct%)` or just total.
  - Pending entries count with the `/rate` hint, when any.
- R23. `/export` sends a CSV of all entries: `date,type,amount,currency,rate,amount_eur,category,note,pot`.

### Monthly summary

- R24. On the 1st at 09:00 Europe/Vienna the bot sends the previous month's report (R22) and the CSV (R23) to Misha. Exactly once per month; if the server was down, it is sent at the next daily run. A month with no entries at all is skipped silently.

### Access, reliability, data

- R25. Phase 1 answers only the owner (Telegram user id in config) in a private chat. Anyone else and any group get no reply and nothing is stored.
- R26. Long polling, IPv4-only connection to Telegram, restart on crash (systemd).
- R27. Nightly database backup on the server, 14 newest kept.
- R28. Data: one SQLite file on the server. Money as integer cents, rates as decimal text. Nothing is sent to third parties.

## Phase 2 - groups (detailed spec written after phase 1 is in use)

Approved scope: bot added to a friends' chat; members register with `/join`; one currency per group set at setup; commands `/paid 60 dinner` (equal split among all), `/paid 60 dinner @anna @max` (equal among payer and listed), `@anna=20 @max=40` (exact amounts), `@anna*2 @max` (shares), `by @anna` (someone else paid), `/balance` (who owes whom, minimum number of transfers), `/settle @anna 20`. Remainder cents distributed deterministically so shares always sum to the total. Group expenses never enter the personal ledger. Privacy mode stays on.

## Phase 3 - Mini App (plan after 2-3 weeks of real data)

Approved scope: charts by category and month, income/expense trend, savings progress, a form for complex group splits. Opens from the bot; needs an HTTPS address (domain on the server or a tunnel), decided at phase start.

## Out of scope

Bank connections, investments, budgets / plan vs actual, LLM parsing, voice, receipt photos, several currencies in one group, the old Excel tracker, custom categories (backlog), USD/UAH pots (backlog).
